About The Working Man

We're on a mission to connect skilled workers with people who need their services, making hiring safe, simple, and reliable.

Our Mission

To create a trusted marketplace where skilled workers can find meaningful work and employers can hire with confidence.

Our Vision

To become the leading platform connecting verified professionals with households and businesses across Kenya and beyond.

Our Promise

Every worker on our platform is verified, rated, and reviewed by real employers, ensuring quality and trust in every match.

Our Story

The Working Man was born from a simple observation: finding reliable, trustworthy workers for everyday tasks shouldn't be difficult or risky.

Whether you need a plumber for an emergency repair, a cleaner for your home, an electrician for rewiring, or a house help for daily chores - the process of finding the right person has traditionally been through word-of-mouth, unverified recommendations, or risky encounters with strangers.

We set out to change that. Our platform brings together skilled workers who are verified, rated, and reviewed by real employers. Every worker profile shows their experience, ratings, work history, and the areas they serve.

For workers, we provide a platform to showcase their skills, build a professional reputation, and connect with employers who value quality work. No more waiting for someone to recommend you - your work speaks for itself through reviews and ratings.

Since launching in 2024, we've helped connect hundreds of workers with employers, completed thousands of jobs, and built a community based on trust, transparency, and quality service.
