How The Working Man Works

Connecting workers and employers through smart matching - simple, fast, and reliable.

1

Create Your Profile

The first step is simple - tell us who you are and what you need.

  • Workers: Create a profile with your skills, experience, expected pay rate, and the areas where you can work. Upload your ID and a selfie for verification.
  • Employers: Register with your name, phone number, and company details (if applicable). It only takes 2 minutes!

💡 Tip: Verified worker profiles get 10 times more job offers!

2

Post or Find Jobs

Now the real action begins! Here's how each side works:

  • Employers post jobs: Describe what you need - the type of worker, location, pay rate, and schedule. Your job becomes instantly visible to all matching workers.
  • Workers browse jobs: See all available jobs in your area. Filter by service type, location, or pay rate. When you find a match, click "Apply" with one button!

💡 Tip: Write a clear, detailed job description to attract the best workers!

3

Smart Matching Happens

This is where the magic happens! Our system automatically finds the best matches based on:

  • Skills Match: The worker's service type matches exactly what the employer needs.
  • Location Match: The worker is available in the employer's area.
  • Budget Match: The worker's expected rate fits within the employer's budget.
  • Rating Priority: Higher-rated, verified workers appear at the top.

💡 The system calculates a "match score" for each worker - the higher the score, the better the match!

Rate, Review & Build Trust

After the job is done, the employer rates the worker. This helps everyone:

  • Good workers earn higher ratings and get more job offers.
  • Employers can see past reviews before hiring.
  • The community becomes safer and more trustworthy for everyone.
  • Poor performers are naturally filtered out by low ratings.

💡 Workers with 4+ star ratings get 5 times more job invitations!

How the Smart Matching Works

Our system calculates a match score for each worker based on four key factors:

40% - Skills Match

Does the worker's service type exactly match what the employer needs? A plumber can't be matched to an electrician job.

30% - Location Match

Is the worker available in the employer's area? Workers select their preferred locations when creating their profile.

20% - Budget Match

Does the worker's expected pay fit within the employer's budget? Workers with rates within range score higher.

10% - Rating Bonus

Higher-rated workers get a bonus. Verified workers get an extra boost. Quality work is rewarded!

A Real Example

Let's see how Mary finds a plumber for her kitchen repair

Step 1: Mary Posts a Job

Mary needs a plumber to fix her kitchen sink in Westlands. She posts a job with: Service: Plumber, Location: Westlands, Pay: $15/hour. The job goes live immediately.

Step 2: Workers See the Job

Three plumbers in Westlands see Mary's job on their dashboard: John (5 years exp, 4.8★, $12/hr), Peter (10 years exp, 4.5★, $18/hr), and James (2 years exp, 3.5★, $10/hr).

Step 3: Smart Matching Calculates Scores

John scores 95% (perfect match - right skills, right location, fits budget, great rating). Peter scores 70% (right skills and location but rate is above budget). James scores 60% (fits budget but has low rating and less experience).

Step 4: Mary Hires John

Mary sees the suggestions sorted by match score. She reviews John's profile, sees his 4.8★ rating and positive reviews, and decides to hire him. She contacts him through the platform's messaging system.

Step 5: Job Complete - Rating Time

John does an excellent job fixing the sink. Mary gives him a 5★ rating and writes: "John was professional, on time, and did great work!" John's rating goes up, helping him get even more jobs in the future.
