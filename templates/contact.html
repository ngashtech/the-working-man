Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as a worker?

Click on "Join as Worker" on the homepage or navigation menu. Fill in your personal details, select your service type, choose your work locations, and upload your ID for verification. It takes less than 5 minutes!

How do I post a job as an employer?

Register as an employer, then click "Post New Job" from your dashboard. Fill in the job title, description, service type needed, location, and pay rate. Your job will be visible to all available workers immediately.

Is the platform free to use?

Yes! The Working Man is completely free for both workers and employers. Workers can create profiles and apply for jobs at no cost. Employers can post jobs and find workers without any fees. We believe in connecting people without barriers.

How does worker verification work?

Workers can upload their National ID, Passport, or Driver's License along with a recent selfie. Our admin team reviews the documents and verifies the worker's identity. Verified workers get a green badge on their profile and are 10x more likely to be hired.

How can I contact customer support?

You can reach us via email at theeworkingman@gmail.com, call or WhatsApp us at +254 745 794 938, or fill in the contact form above. We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days.

What if I have a dispute with a worker/employer?

We encourage open communication between workers and employers to resolve issues. If you cannot reach an agreement, contact our support team at theeworkingman@gmail.com with details of the dispute, and we'll help mediate.
